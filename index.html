AI you can trust

Smart suggestions.
Your final say.

AI never deletes, moves or renames files on its own. Every change goes through a plan you can read and approve.

  1. You ask“Organize my Downloads folder.”
  2. AI interpretsOn-device model reads the intent.
  3. Typed planTurned into a list of concrete actions.
  4. ValidatedPaths, permissions and conflicts checked by code.
  5. You reviewEvery move and its destination, listed.
  6. You approveNothing happens until you click.
  7. ExecutedSafe system APIs. Trash, never erase.
  8. LoggedResults and errors recorded in history.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. AI Search uses Apple’s on-device models when your Mac supports them. Everything else — the image viewer, storage analysis, cleanup, duplicates, text-in-images search and regular file search — works on any supported Mac.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Indexing, text recognition and AI all run on your Mac. MacMate has no cloud processing and needs no account. If a future feature ever needs the internet, it will be clearly labelled and optional.

What can MacMate see on my Mac?

Only the folders you choose. You can add or remove folders at any time, and Settings shows exactly what MacMate has access to. Some tools ask for macOS permissions (Screen Recording for screenshots, Accessibility for window management) only when you turn them on.

Will it delete my files?

Never automatically. Cleanup always shows a preview, asks for confirmation and moves files to the macOS Trash so you can restore them. AI suggestions are only suggestions until you approve.

Does it replace Finder?

No. MacMate works alongside Finder — a visual library, smarter search and handy shortcuts, without changing how Finder works.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple silicon and Intel Macs. AI Search requires a Mac with Apple Intelligence; all core features work without it. Exact macOS requirements will be announced before launch.

How do updates work?

Pro is a one-time purchase that includes every 1.x update. Major versions may be offered as a discounted upgrade; we’ll publish the terms before launch.
