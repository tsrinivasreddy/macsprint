Five ways to browse everything on your Mac

Library shows every file in the folders you chose, sorted by what it is. Every option uses the same numbers as Home (2,880 files; Videos 162 files, 11.21 GB), the decided kind colours, big thumbnails, and the same actions: Space for Quick Look, ⌘I for the inspector, right-click for the menu.

My pick: 1, Kind tabs. One row of tabs (each with its colour dot and count) picks the kind, a line under it says how many files and how much space, and the grid shows big previews. Selecting a file opens the inspector on the right. It is the most Mac-like, works the same for every kind, and is simple to build. 2 (Kind tiles) is the more eye-catching choice if you want Library to repeat the Home storage cards.
