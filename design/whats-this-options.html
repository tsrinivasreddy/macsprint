What’s This? · options

Five ways to say what something is, and whether it is safe to remove

What’s This? lives inside Free Up Space and in the right-click menu (⌥⌘/). It opens on the mystery “System Data” figure and works on any file or folder. Every option uses the same 148 GB and the same three labels: Safe to clear, Check first, Leave it. Labels always pair an icon and words with the colour. Parts macOS will not let any app read are shown, not hidden, so the parts add up. Nothing is removed here: you review files first, they go to the Trash, with Undo.

My pick: 1, Answers in place. A plain list, biggest first, each part with its label. Click “What’s this?” and the answer opens inside the row: what it is, what you would notice, and where the space is. No sheet to dismiss, and the rest of the list stays in view. Right-click answers (option 5) use the same card, so one design covers both.
