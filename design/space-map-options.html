Five ways to see where the space went

Space Map is the third view in Free Up Space (Files, Running total, Map). It answers one question: what is taking up my disk? Every option uses the same disk (245 GB used of 494 GB), colours each folder by the kind of files in it, the same colours as Home, and shows size as area or length, never colour. Nothing is removed from the map itself: you pick something, then review it in Files, with the usual Trash and Undo.

My pick: 1, Nested blocks. Every folder is a block sized by its space, and the biggest folders already show what is inside them, so you see that Trip 2025 is 22 GB without clicking. Click a block to go in; the bar below says what it is and opens it in Files. 2 (Columns) is better when people want exact numbers more than a picture.
