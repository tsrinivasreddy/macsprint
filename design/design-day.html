A one-day course for the MacSprint app

MacSprint Design Day

Go from "what is a sidebar?" to choosing every button, font and screen for MacSprint yourself. Each block has a time, things to learn, the SwiftUI name of what you are learning, a hands-on task for MacSprint and a quick self-check. Tick a block when you finish it; your progress and notes stay in this browser.

0 of 9 done

Assumptions: you are building with SwiftUI for macOS 14 or later, designing in Figma (free plan is enough), and you have about 9 hours including breaks. Change the start time and every block re-times itself.
