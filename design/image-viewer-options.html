Image Viewer · options

Open one photo, then move through the whole folder

Preview on the Mac shows only the photo you opened. MacSprint’s viewer opens any photo, from Finder (Open With) or from Library and Recents, and ← → step through every photo in the same folder. Every option keeps the same keys, so what you learn in one works in all: ← → next and previous, ⌘+ ⌘− zoom, ⌘0 fit, ⌘R rotate, ⌘I details, ⌘⌫ Trash with Undo, Esc back. The photo always sits on a dark stage so colours read true; the window around it follows light or dark.

My pick: 1, Stage and filmstrip. The photo gets almost the whole window, a strip of neighbours shows where you are, and the counter says “16 of 120”. It works the same whether the photo came from Finder or from Library, and Esc goes back to where you came from. 2 (Over the Library) is the better feel when you open photos from inside MacSprint and want to land back on the same spot in the grid.
