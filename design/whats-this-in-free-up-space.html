Where What’s This? fits in the reviewed Free Up Space

These are the reviewed Free Up Space screens, unchanged: the Files view with its six groups, the Review table, and the Map. Each option adds What’s This? in one spot. It looks the same everywhere: a “What’s this?” button just before the row’s main action, and the answer opens under the row with its label (Safe to clear, Check first, Leave it). Right-click and ⌥⌘/ do the same on any selected row. You can choose more than one.

My pick: 1 and 2 together. 1 answers the question at the moment it matters, just before a file goes to the Trash. 2 gives the big “System Data” number a home without adding a sidebar row or a seventh group. 3 and 4 are optional extras.
