Where Paste Clean fits in the reviewed Clipboard

Paste Clean pastes a link without its tracking codes, or text without the fonts and colours it came with. It has no sidebar row: it lives in the reviewed Clipboard panel (⇧⌘V), which is shown here unchanged, and in its own shortcut, ⌥⇧⌘V. Return always pastes exactly what you copied; cleaning is a choice, and you see what it removes before you paste. You can choose more than one.

My pick: 1 and 4 together. In the panel, rows that can be cleaned say so, and the selected one shows exactly what ⌥↩ will remove. Outside the panel, ⌥⇧⌘V pastes the last copy clean straight away, with Undo. 1 is for checking, 4 is for speed; both use the same words and the same result.
