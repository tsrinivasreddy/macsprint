Five search bars you can type or talk to

The search bar is the front door of MacSprint: type a name, describe a file, ask a question about your files, or ask what something is. Every option shows the same moment (“lease” typed, an answer from your files on top, the files below) and, under it, three live states: ready, listening, and asking what a file is. Motion only answers what you do: the bar listens, thinks or opens. With Reduce Motion on, everything stays still.

My pick: 1, Halo. A quiet capsule in the toolbar opens into a floating panel the moment you type or press ⌘F, and a thin ring of colour turns around it only while it listens or thinks. It stays out of the way until you need it, and when it works you can see it working. 4 (Center stage) is the most striking if Home should open on the search bar.
