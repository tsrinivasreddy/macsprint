Five ways to open MacSprint

Home is the first screen people see. Every option uses the final foundations, the same short sidebar and the same numbers: 2,880 files, 30.19 GB, and up to 14.1 GB that could be freed. Search stays the plain centred field here; it turns into the Graphite field once you start searching.

My pick: 1, Space bar. One coloured bar answers “what fills my Mac?” in a second, and the six kinds under it are the doors into Library. Clean Up sits right below with real sizes and one filled button. It reads well in light and dark and stays calm, with no motion. If people open MacSprint mostly to find new files, 2 (Today first) is the better start.
