Five ways to show what is on your Mac

A second round for Home. Each screen is built around one simple graphic, chosen by what the numbers need to say: what fills the Mac, what you can free, what is new, or where files came from. Same sidebar, same toolbar, same numbers as the first round (2,880 files, 30.19 GB, up to 14.1 GB to free).

My pick: 1, Free-up. It leads with the one number people open a Mac cleaner for, shows it against the whole disk, and breaks it into four reasons with sizes. Every bar has its number next to it, so it reads without a legend. Pair it with the Squares from option 2 lower on the same screen for “what fills my Mac”.

Colours. The six file kinds now use a colour order that passed a colour-blindness check in light and dark (the first Home round failed it: Images and Videos looked the same). Every colour also has a text label next to it.
