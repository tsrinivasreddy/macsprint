Free Up Space · round 2

Biggest first, five more ways

You chose “Biggest first”. These keep its order and its promise (review first, files go to the Trash, Undo) and try a stronger idea each. Size always uses one colour: indigo for what to look at, grey for the rest.

My pick: 1, Running total. Each bar starts where the last one ended, so the rows climb like stairs across one 14.1 GB track. You see at once that the first two groups free 8.5 GB, more than half, and that the last three add little. It answers “where should I stop?”, which plain bars cannot. 2 (Show the files) is the one to pick if trust matters most: you see what you are about to review.
