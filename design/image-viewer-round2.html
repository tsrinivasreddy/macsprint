Image Viewer · round 2

Stage and filmstrip, full screen

Option 1 from the first round, now with the photo or video filling the whole screen. The controls float on dark glass over the picture, so they stay readable on bright and dark photos alike, in light and dark mode. Navigation never hides: the ← → buttons stay on screen in every style, the keys always work, and the counter always says where you are. Controls never fade away on a timer; H hides them and H brings them back.

My pick: 1, Floating glass. The picture fills the screen edge to edge, with three small glass pieces on top: where you are (top left), tools (top right) and the filmstrip (bottom). Videos use the same layout with a play bar added (style 4), and tall photos fill the sides with a soft blur of themselves (style 5), so 4 and 5 are how 1 handles videos and portraits, not separate choices.
